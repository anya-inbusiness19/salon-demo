Privacy Policy

This agreement is a plain-language template. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it for your business.

Last updated October 2026

What I collect

  • Anonymous visit data: which page you viewed, which website sent you, your device type (phone, tablet, or computer), and buttons you clicked. A random ID, kept only for your browser session, groups visits together. I don’t collect your name, IP address, or exact location for analytics.
  • Booking details: when you book a Zoom call, the booking tool (Cal.com) collects your name, email, and any notes you add.
  • Reviews: the name, business name, rating, and review you submit.
  • Checkout details: your name, the plan you choose, and your electronic signature. Stripe handles payment details. I never see your full card number.
  • Emails: anything you send me directly.

How I use it

To respond to you, schedule calls, provide and bill for services, keep records of signed agreements, and understand which parts of the site are helpful. I never sell your information.

Services that help run this site

Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (database), Stripe (payments), Cal.com and Zoom (scheduling and calls), and Google Fonts (text styling). Each of these has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one random visit ID in your browser that is deleted when you close the tab. The admin login uses storage only on the admin page.

How long I keep it

Visit data: up to 2 years. Signed agreements and billing records: as long as needed for business and tax records. Reviews: until you ask me to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information by emailing . I’ll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update the date at the top of this page.
